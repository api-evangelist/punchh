Football season is both a cultural phenomenon and a marketer’s dream playbook. With tailgates, office parties, and at-home watch parties driving demand for group dining, it’s the perfect moment to turn casual guests into high-value loyalists. For restaurant marketers looking to capitalize on the season, catering and loyalty should be the MVPs of your strategy.
